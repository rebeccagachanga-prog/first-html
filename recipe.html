Easy Homemade Pizza

This is one of my favourite foods and i am happy to share the recipe
I have baked it multiple times and all have been a success. here is the recipe. Enjoy

Ingredients

Pizza Dough

  • 2 cups all purpose flour
  • 1 tsp intast yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 3/4 cup warm water
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Toppings

  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce or tomato sauce
  • 1-2 cups grated cheese(mozzarella or any melting cheese)
  • Onoin, tomato,capsicum
  • Cooked chicken/beef/sausage(optional)
  • Oregano or mixed herbs(optional)
